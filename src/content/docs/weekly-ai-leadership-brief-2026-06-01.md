## What changed this week

- AI governance failures are costing enterprises up to $735 per employee annually due to inefficiencies and risks (source 4).
- Agentic AI is moving from concept to enterprise deployment, with leaders like NVIDIA partnering with software firms to build autonomous agents (source 10).
- Data governance maturity models are creating false confidence; true AI readiness requires deep business semantics, not just data maturity (source 5).

## Leadership implications

- Executives must move beyond CFO-only AI investment decisions; cross-functional leadership is essential for effective AI strategy (source 9).
- A clear AI vision is needed to break innovation ceilings; leaders must articulate how AI transforms business models (source 7).
- Security-first governance is non-negotiable as AI expands attack surfaces; governance must be embedded from the start (source 12).

## Decisions for the next 7 days

- Assess your current AI governance framework against the $735 problem: identify and address inefficiencies in decision rights and accountability (source 4).
- Initiate a review of your operating model to accommodate agentic AI, focusing on human-AI collaboration and new workflows (source 8).
- Ensure your data strategy includes business semantics, not just technical metadata, to avoid false readiness (source 2).
