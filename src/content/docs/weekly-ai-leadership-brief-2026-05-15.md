## What changed this week

- 80% of CEOs worldwide believe AI could cost them their job, highlighting the personal stakes for leaders (source 6).
- Enterprise AI adoption is outpacing existing infrastructure and governance models, creating a 'make-or-break' moment for IT leadership (sources 5, 7).
- Agentic AI workflows are emerging, requiring new guardrails like the AEGIS framework to ensure security and control (sources 4, 8).

## Leadership implications

- CEOs must personally champion AI governance to avoid being displaced; this is a leadership imperative, not just a technical issue (source 6).
- CHROs need to redefine human capital strategies to integrate AI skills and manage workforce transitions (source 2).
- Organizations must assess their 'AI readiness' and adapt operating models to move from ambition to frontier transformation (source 3).

## Decisions for the next 7 days

- Initiate a board-level review of AI governance frameworks, referencing the Responsible AI Governance practical framework (source 1).
- Evaluate current infrastructure and governance models against the pace of AI adoption; plan for upgrades to avoid being outpaced (source 7).
- Pilot agentic AI workflows with strict guardrails, using frameworks like AEGIS to manage risks (source 8).
