## What changed this week

- SAS makes AI governance the centerpiece of its agent strategy, signaling a shift toward integrated governance in AI platforms.
- New research emphasizes that connected data creates a 'trust dividend' for AI-driven sustainability decisions, but many organizations lack data integration.
- AI adoption continues to outpace governance frameworks, with boards facing pressure to prevent AI washing and manage cybersecurity risks.

## Leadership implications

- Executives must prioritize data connectivity and quality to make AI decision-ready, especially for sustainability and operational use cases.
- AI governance should be embedded into the operating model, not treated as an afterthought, to keep pace with adoption.
- Boards need to establish clear oversight of AI claims and risks to avoid reputational damage from AI washing or security breaches.

## Decisions for the next 7 days

- Assess your organization's data integration maturity and identify gaps between AI initiatives and enterprise systems.
- Review AI governance policies to ensure they cover agent-based AI, data risk, and alignment with business strategy.
- Initiate a board-level discussion on AI risk appetite, including cybersecurity and compliance with emerging regulations.
