## What changed this week

- The technology executive role is expanding under AI pressure, requiring broader oversight beyond traditional IT (Source 3).
- Persistent and IIM Ahmedabad launched 'AI Value Compass' to bring discipline and measurable outcomes to enterprise AI investments (Source 2).
- Kore.ai unveiled Artemis, a platform to build, govern, and optimize enterprise AI agents (Source 7).

## Leadership implications

- 70% of workers say they are ready for AI, but only 39% of leaders agree, indicating a perception gap that may hinder adoption (Source 6).
- Scaling AI requires both left-brain rigor (data, governance) and right-brain ingenuity (creativity, experimentation) (Source 5).
- Real enterprise transformation with AI requires six foundations, not just technology; leaders must address all (Source 4).

## Decisions for the next 7 days

- Assess your enterprise stack readiness: AI adoption problems often stem from underlying infrastructure, not the AI itself (Source 9).
- Budget for hidden AI costs: include data preparation, model maintenance, governance, and change management (Source 10).
- Establish an AI governance framework to ensure responsible agent deployment and measurable outcomes (Source 7, 2).
