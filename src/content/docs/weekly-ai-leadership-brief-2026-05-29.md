## What changed this week

- AI governance is now a boardroom imperative, with experts warning that ignoring it risks significant financial and reputational damage (Source 1).
- Agentic AI breaks traditional operating models, requiring a fundamental redesign of how enterprises structure teams, processes, and decision rights (Source 2).
- A survey reveals 70% of workers feel ready for AI, but only 39% of leaders believe they are, indicating a perception gap that could slow adoption (Source 11).

## Leadership implications

- CEOs must champion a clear AI vision to break innovation ceilings; without it, competitive advantage erodes (Source 4).
- Enterprise AI governance is often set up to fail due to fragmented ownership and lack of integration with existing risk frameworks (Source 3).
- Security-first governance is non-negotiable as agentic AI introduces new vulnerabilities that traditional security models cannot address (Sources 6, 7).

## Decisions for the next 7 days

- Establish a cross-functional AI governance committee with C-suite sponsorship to oversee risk, compliance, and ethics (Source 1).
- Initiate a review of your operating model to identify where agentic AI can be integrated and where decision rights need to shift (Source 2).
- Mandate a FinOps review of AI spending to ensure cost transparency and alignment with business value (Source 8).
