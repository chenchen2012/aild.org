## What changed this week

- IBM survey: CEOs expect AI to make 48% of routine decisions by 2030, but governance lags.
- Anthropic's powerful AI model exposed corporate governance gaps; a new framework is needed.
- OneStream reports companies scale AI on data they don't trust, undermining reliability.
- Collibra launched AI Command Center for real-time oversight of agentic AI.
- Demand for AI is outpacing the scaffolding (governance, data, operating model) to support it.

## Leadership implications

- CEOs must establish clear accountability for AI decisions, as AI spreads decision-making but not responsibility.
- Data governance and trust are foundational; without trusted data, AI scaling is risky.
- A unified operating model (one team, one platform) is essential for AI scalability.
- Real-time oversight tools (like Collibra) are emerging to manage agentic AI risks.
- CEOs should adopt a framework for AI governance that includes ethical guidelines and risk controls.

## Decisions for the next 7 days

- Review your AI governance framework: ensure accountability for AI-driven decisions is assigned to specific roles.
- Audit data trustworthiness across AI pipelines; address data quality issues before scaling.
- Evaluate your AI operating model: consider consolidating to one team and one platform for consistency.
- Assess the need for real-time AI oversight tools to monitor agentic AI behavior.
- Initiate a cross-functional task force to develop an AI decision-making accountability charter.
