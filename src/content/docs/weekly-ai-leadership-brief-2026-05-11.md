## What changed this week

- Nearly half of large enterprises lack full visibility into employee AI use, highlighting a governance gap (Source 9).
- Experts emphasize that AI success depends on more than models—data governance and operating models are key (Sources 3, 8).
- New frameworks like AEGIS propose enterprise guardrails for securing agentic AI (Source 11).

## Leadership implications

- Executives must build AI fluency to make informed decisions, not just adopt tools (Source 1).
- AI is spreading decision-making but not accountability; leaders must clarify ownership (Source 2).
- Data exposure risks in generative AI require proactive governance, not afterthought (Source 4).

## Decisions for the next 7 days

- Audit current AI usage across the organization to identify visibility gaps (Source 9).
- Define a unified AI operating model with clear accountability for decisions (Sources 2, 8).
- Implement guardrails for agentic AI, starting with data exposure policies (Sources 4, 11).
