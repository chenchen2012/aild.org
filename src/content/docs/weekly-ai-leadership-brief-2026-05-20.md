## What changed this week

- Enterprise AI adoption is outpacing existing infrastructure and governance models, creating risk (NTT DATA).
- Gartner identifies information integrity as the top risk concern for enterprise leaders, highlighting governance gaps.
- CIOs and CHROs must collaborate to retain AI talent, as competition for skilled professionals intensifies.

## Leadership implications

- AI governance policies lag behind employee tool usage; executives must prioritize policy updates to manage risk.
- Data readiness is a prerequisite for AI; leaders should assess data quality and infrastructure before scaling AI.
- Cross-functional leadership (CIO-CHRO partnership) is essential for building and retaining AI talent.

## Decisions for the next 7 days

- Initiate a review of current AI governance policies to align with actual employee AI tool usage.
- Conduct a data readiness audit to identify gaps in data quality and infrastructure for AI initiatives.
- Schedule a joint CIO-CHRO meeting to develop a talent retention strategy for AI roles.
