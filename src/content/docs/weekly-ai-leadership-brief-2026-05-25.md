## What changed this week

- Persistent and IIM Ahmedabad launched the 'AI Value Compass' to bring discipline and measurable outcomes to enterprise AI investments (source 2).
- Research shows AI adoption is primarily an organizational change problem, not a technology problem (source 3).
- New data reveals senior leaders are the biggest source of shadow AI risk in organizations (sources 7, 10).

## Leadership implications

- Executives must treat AI adoption as a change management initiative, requiring cross-functional governance and cultural shifts (source 3).
- The technology executive role is expanding under AI pressure, demanding broader oversight of data, trust, and ethics (source 5).
- Digital trust is becoming core to AI governance; leaders must prioritize transparency and accountability (source 6).

## Decisions for the next 7 days

- Assess your organization's data readiness using the seven signs outlined in source 4 to avoid AI pitfalls.
- Establish a cross-functional AI governance committee to address shadow AI risks, especially from senior leaders (sources 7, 10).
- Initiate collaboration between CIO and CHRO to develop AI talent retention strategies (source 11).
