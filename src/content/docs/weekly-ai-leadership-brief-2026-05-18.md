## What changed this week

- 80% of CEOs worldwide believe AI could cost them their job, highlighting the pressure on leadership to adapt (Source 2).
- BPM successfully scaled a Copilot culture from pilots to firmwide impact, demonstrating a path for high-stakes AI adoption (Source 3).
- Gartner identifies information integrity as the top risk concern for enterprise leaders, emphasizing the need for robust AI governance (Source 9).

## Leadership implications

- CEOs must proactively reshape their role and operating model to leverage AI, or risk being replaced (Source 2).
- Scaling AI requires a cultural shift, not just technology deployment; leaders must foster a 'Copilot culture' (Source 3).
- Information integrity is a critical governance issue; leaders must invest in data quality and AI risk management (Source 9).

## Decisions for the next 7 days

- Assess your organization's AI readiness and identify gaps in governance and risk management frameworks (Source 5, 10).
- Initiate a review of your AI portfolio to move from isolated pilots to integrated, enterprise-wide solutions (Source 3).
- Engage CHRO to redefine human capital strategies for an AI-augmented workforce (Source 1).
